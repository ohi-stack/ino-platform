# INO Platform Capability Specification

## Product Definition

The INO Platform Plugin is the intended digital operating system for the Indigenous Nation of Onegodia. It is not limited to membership management. Its architecture is intended to unify public information, member services, identity and heritage preservation, community engagement, programs, volunteers, housing, treasury and grants, governance, records, certificates, communications, reporting, and administrative controls.

## Operational Status Rule

A capability may be described as **operational** only when it is:

1. Fully implemented.
2. Documented.
3. Tested.
4. Permission-controlled.
5. Repeatable in a production WordPress installation.

Capabilities not meeting all five requirements must be labeled **planned**, **in development**, or **experimental**.

## 4. Identity and Heritage

Users should be able to:

- Record ancestral heritage.
- Document family lineage.
- Build a family tree.
- Upload genealogy records.
- Add oral histories.
- Upload photographs.
- Join family groups.
- Search the People’s Book of Names.
- View Tribal and Family Archives.
- Generate heritage certificates.

Identity declarations must distinguish among:

- Self-declared.
- Family-attested.
- Document-supported.
- Institutionally reviewed.
- Pending documentation.
- Unverified.

The platform must preserve a clear distinction among ancestry, INO membership, and external governmental or tribal recognition.
