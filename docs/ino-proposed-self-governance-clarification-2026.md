## Platform workflow

1. Governance reviewer verifies controlling governing instruments, amendment rules and delegated roles.
2. Records officer creates a DRAFT meeting with a real proposed date if scheduled; adds an agenda item before scheduling.
3. Authorized officer records actual meeting held with attendance and source evidence; the platform does not infer quorum.
4. Records officer submits minute revision with documentary source reference; independent authorized reviewer reviews the latest version.
5. Records officer creates a resolution draft with exact motion text and `§ 33-264c(b)` and internal authority references; another authorized reviewer reviews it.
6. A separate authorized publisher may record the reported voting outcome with real quorum and decision evidence; `outcome_recorded` **does not equal legally adopted**.
7. Counsel/authorized governance personnel independently verify legal adoption, effective date, amendment text, authentic signatures, and whether public publication is permissible.
8. Only after separate proof of adoption may an appropriately classified *public* governance record be reviewed/published. The ODIN public registry cannot treat a Phase 2 reported outcome as adopted legislation and must preserve its own verification limitations.
9. Retain private original minutes, member vote records, signatures and attendance outside the publicly accessible WordPress Media Library.
10. Preserve original OAS statements as separately attributed primary sources with bibliographic page references, not rewritten declarations of INO jurisdiction.

## 10 permitted leadership uses
Constitutional governance; governing meetings; membership; voluntary dispute resolution; agency presentations; lawful land stewardship; rights advocacy; inter-Indigenous cooperation; housing and grants; and authenticated institutional records. None of these creates territorial title, compulsory jurisdiction, public government or exemption from applicable law.
